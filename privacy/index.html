Privacy Policy

If I Were Korean · Effective 23 November 2026

If I Were Korean (“the app”, “we”) reads your Korean birth chart (saju) and writes a Korean name for you. We designed it to need as little of your information as possible. This policy explains what the app uses, what leaves your device, and the choices you have.

In short
  • Your name, birth date, birth time, birthplace and gender stay on your device. They are never sent to our servers.
  • We don’t ask for your email, phone number or real name, and we don’t use ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Our servers keep only what is needed to make your purchases work: an anonymous account ID, your purchase records, and a one-way code for each unlocked reading.
  • You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

1. Information that stays on your device

To read your chart, you enter a name, birth date, birth time (optional), country, city and gender. The app calculates your chart, reading and Korean names on your device, without an internet connection. These details are kept in the app’s memory while you use it and are not uploaded.

When you tap “Listen”, your device’s built-in speech voice reads the name aloud; no audio is recorded. When you share a reading, the app creates an image on your device and hands it to the share sheet you choose.

2. Information our servers receive

Only the paid features need a server, so that a purchase can be confirmed, kept and restored.

WhatWhy
Anonymous account IDA random ID created when the app first connects. It lets us know which purchases are yours. It is not linked to your name or email.
Purchase recordsWhich product you bought, when, and the App Store transaction ID, so we can grant, restore or refund your unlocks.
Reading codeWhen you unlock a reading, the app sends a one-way code (a SHA-256 hash) made from that person’s birth details. It lets the same person be unlocked only once and restored later. We cannot turn it back into a name or a birth date, and we never receive the details themselves.
Gift codesCodes you create from a 3-pack, and whether they have been redeemed.
Apple ID identifier (optional)Only if you choose Sign in with Apple. We receive an Apple user identifier so your purchases can follow you to a new device. We do not request your name or email from Apple.
Technical dataLike any internet service, our providers process your IP address and basic device information (such as device model and OS version) to deliver requests and prevent abuse.

3. Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run the paid features. They process data on our behalf and only for these purposes:

  • Apple — App Store payments and optional Sign in with Apple. Apple handles your payment details; we never see them. See Apple’s privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat — confirms App Store purchases. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.
  • Supabase — hosts our database and account sign-in. See Supabase’s privacy policy.

We do not sell your information, share it for advertising, or use it to track you across other apps or websites.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account and purchase records for as long as your account exists, so your unlocks can be restored. When you delete your account, we delete it together with your unlocked readings and unused gift codes. Records of completed App Store transactions are kept with your account removed from them, so they can no longer be linked to you; we need them for accounting and to handle refunds.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: open Account & purchases in the app and tap Delete my data (or Delete account if you signed in). This deletes your account, unlocked readings, unused credits and unused gift codes, and, if you used Sign in with Apple, revokes the app’s access to your Apple ID. Gift codes someone else already redeemed keep working for them.
  • Sign out: you can sign out at any time; your purchases stay with your Apple ID.
  • Access or questions: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or privacy laws in California or Korea), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, or to object to its processing. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Security

Data sent to our servers travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The app cannot read our database directly; our server only returns the records of the signed-in account.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app.
